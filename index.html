COLLECTION BOUNDARIES

What this page cannot see

  • Raw capacitive electrode matrices or sensor voltages.
  • Fingerprint ridges, biometric templates, or hardware touch-controller data.
  • Physical force calibration from a browser pressure value.
  • Reliable person identity from a gesture or set of browser signals.
  • Signals blocked, normalized, quantized, or synthesized by the OS/browser.
06

Research protocol

REPEATABLE TRIALS · CONTROLLED CONDITIONS
SUGGESTED SEQUENCE

Run the same gesture more than once

  1. 01
    Single tap

    Tap once without dragging; repeat 5–10 times with a consistent pause.

  2. 02
    Long press

    Hold still for approximately two seconds, then release.

  3. 03
    Slow / fast drag

    Trace a straight line at two comfortable speeds; keep the start and end zones consistent.

  4. 04
    Shapes

    Draw small circles, then straight lines in the same part of the field.

  5. 05
    Regular repeated taps

    Tap to a steady rhythm; compare measured intervals, not just visual impressions.

  6. 06
    Multi-touch

    Use two contacts only when the device/browser supports it; compare distance changes.

INTERPRETATION NOTES

Keep comparisons honest

Control the environment

Use the same device, browser, orientation, page zoom, finger, and gesture instructions. Record interruptions, screen protectors, moisture, and whether the input was a finger, stylus, mouse, or trackpad.

Compare repeat trials

Export each session JSON and label each trial. Compare distributions and sample counts across sessions. A small sample or one unusually fast / slow gesture is not enough to establish a stable pattern.

Understand timing

Event timestamps are browser timing values, not a guarantee of physical sensor scan timing. Main-thread load, coalescing, event dispatch, and browser scheduling can change observed intervals.

Interpret pressure carefully

Some devices report fixed or synthetic pressure (often 0.5 while in contact). Missing, constant, or coarse values can be normal. Dimensions may represent an estimated contact ellipse or implementation-specific values.

Privacy by default

Samples stay in page memory. Export files may contain high-resolution interaction traces; store and share them carefully. This page does not send samples to a server or persist them in local storage.
