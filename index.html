How this works

Touch Lab compares your contact duration, movement, reported contact size, pressure and other browser-visible signals. It needs labelled examples because a webpage cannot directly read which physical finger made a touch. Predictions are estimates, not identity verification.

A held-out-batch score will appear after enough examples have been collected. It may not generalise to another phone or session.
